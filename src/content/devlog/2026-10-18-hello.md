Hello, and welcome. We are Twin Pixels, a small independent studio, and this is the first page of our devlog.

## What we are making

Two things, built side by side. **Kindred** is our first game, a world we want you to get lost in. **Driftstone** is the engine underneath it, which we are building ourselves so the game can be exactly what we want it to be.

That is all we are going to say about either for now. We would rather show you something finished than describe something half-built.

## What to expect here

A short note every so often, written by us, about things you can see or play: what changed, what it feels like, what is next. When there is something worth looking at, there will be a picture or a clip. We will not walk through how the work gets done.

If you found this page, you are early. Thank you for being here.

If you want to say hello, write to [info@twinpixels.gg](mailto:info@twinpixels.gg).
